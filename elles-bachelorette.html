House rules

Straight from the VacayAZ listing and rental agreement. Give them a read before we ride.

  • Quiet after 9 pm. Noise meters on site tattle to the host, so keep the music and games to a respectful roar.
  • No tape on walls or ceilings. The balloon arch and backdrop have to stand on their own.
  • No smoking anywhere on the property. The fee is $500.
  • No cannabis of any kind. The stay can be cancelled with no refund.
  • No parties or large gatherings without host approval, and no more guests than the listing allows.
  • No pets.
  • Cameras. There may be security cameras outside, likely a doorbell camera at the front door. The host can see who comes and goes.
  • Report damage within 1 hour of check-in. After that the house counts as in good condition, and guests are charged for any damage during the stay.
  • Checkout by 10 am. Leave the house broom clean with everything packed. Items left behind cost $25 plus shipping.
